Privacy Policy

How We Collect & Use Your Information

1. Introduction

WorkPalNG ("we", "our", or "us") operates a platform that connects skilled artisans and service professionals with clients who need their services across Nigeria. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using WorkPalNG — including our website, mobile application, and all related services — you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly and information generated through your use of our platform:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, password, and account type (artisan or client).
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, bio, skills, service categories, portfolio images/videos, work experience, and certifications.
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates or address used to match you with nearby artisans or clients through our location-based discovery feature.
  • Job & Service Data: Job postings, budgets, milestones, deliverables, applications, quotes, and project details.
  • Communication Data: Messages sent through our direct messaging (chat) system, including text, images, and attachments.
  • Content Data: Posts, stories, comments, likes, shares, and any media you upload to the work feed.
  • Payment Information: Payment method preferences and transaction history related to services arranged on the platform.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, pages visited, and usage patterns.
  • Notification Preferences: Your choices regarding push notifications, email alerts, and in-app notifications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Creating, verifying, and managing your WorkPalNG account and profile.
  • Matching clients with artisans based on location, skills, availability, and service categories.
  • Facilitating direct messaging and communication between users.
  • Displaying your posts, stories, and portfolio content on the work feed.
  • Processing and managing job postings, applications, quotes, and project workflows.
  • Sending real-time notifications about messages, job requests, likes, and platform updates.
  • Providing analytics and insights to artisans about their profile views, engagement, and performance.
  • Improving, personalizing, and optimizing the platform experience.
  • Enforcing our Terms & Conditions, preventing fraud, and ensuring platform security.
  • Complying with legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

  • With Other Users: Your public profile, portfolio, ratings, and reviews are visible to other platform users.
  • Service Providers: We use trusted third-party services (e.g., Firebase, cloud hosting, analytics) to operate the platform. These providers access data only as needed to perform their functions.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by Nigerian law, court order, or government regulation.
  • Safety & Protection: We may share information to protect the rights, property, or safety of WorkPalNG, our users, or the public.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. If you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize your data within 90 days, unless we are required by law to retain it longer. Aggregated, non-identifiable data may be retained for analytics and platform improvement.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate profile information at any time via your account settings.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting our support team.
  • Opt-Out: Manage your notification preferences or unsubscribe from promotional communications.
  • Data Portability: Request your data in a portable format where technically feasible.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at 09138567705.

7. Children's Privacy

WorkPalNG is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 16, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

Cookie Policy

How We Use Cookies & Similar Technologies

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website or use our platform. They help us recognize your device, remember your preferences, and improve your experience. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and device identifiers used in our mobile application.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function — login sessions, security tokens, and authentication state. These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences such as language, theme (dark mode), and notification settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with WorkPalNG — which pages are visited, how long users spend on features, and where errors occur. We use this data to improve the platform.
  • Performance Cookies: Monitor platform speed and performance to ensure a smooth experience for all users.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Firebase that set their own cookies. These services help us analyze usage patterns and improve performance. Third-party cookies are governed by the respective provider's privacy policy. We do not allow third-party advertising cookies on our platform.

4. Managing Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may prevent you from using certain features of WorkPalNG. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete existing cookies.
  • Block all or specific cookies.
  • Set preferences for certain websites.
  • Enable "Do Not Track" signals (though not all services honor this).

Security Policy

How We Protect Your Data

1. Our Commitment to Security

At WorkPalNG, we take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our security practices are regularly reviewed and updated to address emerging threats.

2. Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: Data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL protocols.
  • Authentication: We use Firebase Authentication with secure token-based sessions to protect your account.
  • Database Security: Our Firestore database is protected by strict security rules that enforce access control based on user roles and ownership.
  • Password Protection: User passwords are hashed and salted — they are never stored in plain text.
  • Secure APIs: All API endpoints are authenticated and rate-limited to prevent abuse.

3. Access Controls

Access to user data within WorkPalNG is strictly limited. Only authorized team members with a legitimate business need can access backend systems. We follow the principle of least privilege — team members are granted only the minimum level of access required to perform their duties.

4. Data Breach Response

In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will:

  • Investigate and contain the breach as quickly as possible.
  • Notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.
  • Report the incident to relevant Nigerian data protection authorities as required by law.
  • Take corrective action to prevent future occurrences.

5. Reporting Vulnerabilities

If you discover a security vulnerability in WorkPalNG, please report it responsibly by contacting us on WhatsApp at 09138567705. We appreciate the security research community's efforts and will work quickly to address any confirmed vulnerabilities. Please do not publicly disclose the issue until we have had an opportunity to address it.

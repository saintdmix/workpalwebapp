Terms & Conditions

Rules for using WorkPal

1. Account responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of your profile, the security of your account, and any activity that occurs under your login.

2. Acceptable use

Do not use WorkPal for fraud, harassment, impersonation, spam, illegal activity, or content that violates other users' rights.

Unless a specific WorkPal feature says otherwise, clients and artisans agree on pricing, schedules, and payment terms directly. We are not responsible for off-platform payment disputes.

4. Content and moderation

You keep ownership of your content, but you give WorkPal permission to display and process it as needed to operate the platform. We may remove content or suspend accounts that violate these rules.

5. Service changes

We may update features, pricing, or policies from time to time. Continued use of WorkPal means you accept the updated terms once they are posted.

FAQs

Common questions

How does WorkPal work? v

Clients can search for artisans, post jobs, or contact professionals directly. Artisans can build a profile, showcase their work, and respond to requests that match their skills.

Is my personal information shared publicly? v

Only the details you choose to include in your public profile are shown to other users. Sensitive account information is used to operate the platform and is not shown publicly.

Do you handle payments between clients and artisans? v

In most cases, WorkPal connects the two parties and payment terms are agreed directly between them. If we introduce an in-app payment flow later, we will explain it clearly inside the product.

How do I delete my account? v

Contact support on WhatsApp and request account deletion. We may ask you to confirm ownership before removing the account and related data.

What should I do if I have a dispute? v

Try to resolve the issue directly with the other party first. If you still need help, contact support with the details and we will review the situation where appropriate.

How can I contact WorkPal? v

The fastest option is WhatsApp at 09138567705. You can also call the number directly from the contact buttons on this page.
